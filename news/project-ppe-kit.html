Project PPE Kits: Helmet, Vest, Shoes and Gloves in One Order

Ordering a full kit per worker is simpler than four separate orders — and it keeps the whole team matched.

Start with headcount. One helmet, one vest, one pair of shoes and one pair of gloves per worker is the usual kit; adjust the glove and shoe styles to the job.

Match colours across the kit so the team reads as one crew on site. Confirm the colour range for each item at inquiry — not every style comes in every colour.

Place one combined inquiry listing all four items and the quantities. The supplier can then quote the kit together and confirm reports for each item from its supplying factory.
