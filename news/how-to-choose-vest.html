How to Choose a Reflective Vest

Fabric, fit and stripe layout decide whether a vest gets worn. Match the vest to the job, not just the colour.

Mesh breathes better on hot days; solid polyester holds its shape and is easier to clean. For warehouse and road work in heat, buyers often start with mesh.

Pockets matter when the wearer carries tools, a radio or paperwork. A basic vest is lighter and cheaper; a pocket vest keeps hands free.

Check the fit over work clothes, not over a T-shirt. The vest should close at the front, sit straight, and leave the reflective stripes visible from the front and back.
